How cashless admission works

  1. Bring your policy card or Jan Arogya card and a photo ID at admission.
  2. Our TPA desk files the pre-authorisation request the same day.
  3. Insurer approval usually arrives within 6–24 hours; emergency care is never delayed for it.
  4. At discharge you pay only the non-payable components, itemised in writing.
