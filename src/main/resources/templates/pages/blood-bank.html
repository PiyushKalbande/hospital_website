Need blood urgently?

Call the emergency desk first: 108 or 108 / +91 70836 82563. Do not wait for this form.
  • Donors must be 18–60 years, above 50 kg, and free of infection.
  • Whole-blood donation interval is 90 days for men, 120 days for women.
  • Our donor register is used only for matching requests — never shared commercially.
