Tertiary care that stays in the taluka

Chinche Hospital began in 1998 as a twelve-bed nursing home on Mulai Road. Today it is a 120-bed multispeciality hospital with an intensive care unit, four operating theatres, a dialysis unit, a neonatal unit and an NABL-accredited laboratory — all within Katol.

The reason is simple. For decades, a family here with a heart attack, a fracture or a complicated pregnancy had to travel 55 kilometres to Nagpur. That journey costs money, costs time, and sometimes costs a life. We built the capability here so that journey is no longer necessary.

Our consultants live in the region or visit on fixed weekly days. Our nursing staff are largely from Katol taluka itself. Our reports are explained in Marathi, because a report nobody understands is not care.

What we are accountable for

Published price estimates before every elective surgery — no surprise billing.
Infection-control audits and biomedical waste compliance reviewed quarterly.
A patient grievance cell that must acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours.
Cashless treatment under Jan Arogya Yojana and most major TPAs.
